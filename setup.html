Setup guide

Everything you need to get FirmwareNerds Poster posting on your LinkedIn profile, step by step. It takes about 30 minutes.

Thank you for buying FirmwareNerds Poster. Once it's set up, it writes and publishes one post a day about your field on your LinkedIn profile, at 07:00 Harare time. It works for any field: tech, farming, property, finance, health, marketing, education and more. It runs on your own free GitHub account and uses your own free Gemini and LinkedIn keys. Nobody else, including FirmwareNerds, ever sees your passwords or keys.

Setup takes about 30 minutes. Follow the steps in order. Need help? Contact FirmwareNerds on WhatsApp; the number is on the product page.

What it posts

Day Post
Monday News roundup: 3 recent stories from your field and why they matter
Tuesday One concept from your field explained simply, with an everyday analogy
Wednesday One practical tip people in your field can use the same day
Thursday Your take on one news story
Friday A question about a real debate in your field, to get comments
Saturday A tool or resource of the week
Sunday A career or mindset lesson from your own perspective

Every post is 900–1,300 characters, opens with a hook, ends with a question and has 3–5 hashtags. It never repeats a topic from your last 14 posts.

Your field comes from the AUTHOR_NICHE setting (step 4). If you leave it empty, the posts are about technology.

Where the news comes from

  • With a field set: recent stories found by Google News searches for your field, plus Reuters stories on the same topic. Put your country in the field (for example real estate in Zimbabwe) to get local news.
  • Without a field (tech): TechCrunch, Wired, CNET, The Verge, Ars Technica, Reuters technology and Hacker News.
  • Any extra feeds you like: add RSS feed links in the EXTRA_FEEDS setting (step 4).

The posts only state what the headlines say, and never invent facts.

What it costs to run: nothing. Each run takes about one minute of the 2,000 free GitHub Actions minutes a month, and Gemini and LinkedIn are free.

Step 1. Get your own copy

  1. If you don't have a GitHub account, create one for free at https://github.com/signup.
  2. Open the invitation email from GitHub (sent after your payment was confirmed) and click View invitation → Accept invitation. If you can't find the email, go to https://github.com/bigwellbara/firmwarenerds-poster-agent/invitations.
  3. On the repository page, click the green Use this template button, then Create a new repository.
  4. Set:
    • Owner: your own account
    • Repository name: for example my-linkedin-poster
    • Private (important)
  5. Click Create repository. From now on, work only in your new repository.

Step 2. Get a free Gemini API key

  1. Go to https://aistudio.google.com/apikey and sign in with a Google account.
  2. Click Create API key, then choose or create a project. No billing is needed.
  3. Copy the key.

Step 3. Set up LinkedIn

3a. Create a LinkedIn Company Page (if you don't have one)

LinkedIn requires every developer app to be linked to a Company Page. Your posts still go to your personal profile.

  1. On LinkedIn, click For Business → Create a Company Page.
  2. Fill in a name (your brand or "YourName Dev"), a unique address, your website if you have one, and an industry (click it in the dropdown). Choose size 0-1 employees and type Self-employed.
  3. Tick the confirmation box and click Create page.

3b. Create the LinkedIn app

  1. Go to https://www.linkedin.com/developers/apps and click Create app.
  2. Enter an app name, for example My Daily Poster. Choose your Company Page in LinkedIn Page, upload any square logo, tick the agreement and click Create app.
  3. If the Settings tab shows Verify, click it and approve it as the page admin.

3c. Add two products

On the app's Products tab, click Request access on:

  1. Share on LinkedIn
  2. Sign In with LinkedIn using OpenID Connect

Ignore every other product.

3d. Generate your token

  1. Go to https://www.linkedin.com/developers/tools/oauth/token-generator and select your app.
  2. Tick openid, profile and w_member_social.
  3. Click Request access token, sign in, and click Allow.
  4. Copy the access token.

If it complains about the redirect URL: on the app's Auth tab, add https://www.linkedin.com/developers/tools/oauth/redirect under Authorized redirect URLs, then try again.

Step 4. Add your keys and your bio to GitHub

In your repository, go to Settings → Secrets and variables → Actions.

Secrets tab → New repository secret, twice:

Name Value
GEMINI_API_KEY Your Gemini key from step 2
LINKEDIN_ACCESS_TOKEN Your LinkedIn token from step 3d

Variables tab → New repository variable:

Name Value
AUTHOR_BIO Required. One sentence describing you. The posts are written in this voice. Example: An estate agent in Harare who helps families buy their first home.
AUTHOR_NICHE Recommended. Your field. Every post and every news story follows it, so your posts are about what your audience cares about. Add your country for local news. Examples: real estate in Zimbabwe, agriculture and farming, fintech and mobile money, accounting and tax, cybersecurity. Leave it empty for technology posts.

If you skip AUTHOR_BIO, the posts describe a senior software engineer in Harare, which is probably not you.

Step 5. Test it (nothing is posted)

  1. Open the Actions tab. If GitHub asks, click I understand my workflows, go ahead and enable them.
  2. Click Daily LinkedIn Post on the left, then Run workflow. Leave Dry run ticked and click the green Run workflow button.
  3. When it finishes (about 1–2 minutes), click the run. The draft post is shown in the Summary.

In the run's log, check for:

  • Writing as: followed by your bio, and Niche: followed by your field;
  • LinkedIn author: urn:li:person:..., which means your LinkedIn token works.

Step 6. Go live

You don't need to do anything else: from tomorrow, a post is published every day at 07:00 Harare time. To post straight away, run the workflow again with Dry run unticked.

It never posts more than once a day. To pause it, go to Actions → Daily LinkedIn Post → ⋯ → Disable workflow.

Step 7. Renew your LinkedIn token every 60 days

LinkedIn tokens expire after about 60 days. When yours expires, the daily run fails and GitHub emails you. To fix it, repeat step 3d and paste the new token into the LINKEDIN_ACCESS_TOKEN secret (step 4). Set a calendar reminder for 55 days from today.

Optional settings (Variables tab)

Variable Default What it does
AUTHOR_BIO Harare senior software engineer Who the posts are written as. Set this.
AUTHOR_NICHE (none: technology) Your field. Every topic and news story follows it.
EXTRA_FEEDS (none) Extra news sources: RSS feed links, separated by spaces or commas.
GEMINI_MODEL gemini-flash-lite-latest Main Gemini model.
GEMINI_FALLBACK_MODEL gemini-flash-latest Used if the main model fails.
LINKEDIN_VERSION 202610 LinkedIn API version. The agent tries newer months automatically if this one is retired.

To change the posting time, edit the cron line in .github/workflows/daily-post.yml. It's in UTC, so Harare time minus 2 hours.

Troubleshooting

What you see Fix
LinkedIn HTTP 401 The token expired or was pasted wrongly. Repeat step 3d and update the secret.
LinkedIn HTTP 403 The token is missing a scope, or a product wasn't added. Check steps 3c and 3d.
Gemini API key not valid Create a new key (step 2) and update GEMINI_API_KEY.
Gemini 429 or 503 Google is busy or you hit the free limit. The agent retries and switches model automatically; if it still fails, it tries again tomorrow.
Writing as: shows the wrong person Set the AUTHOR_BIO variable (step 4).
No post at exactly 07:00 GitHub sometimes starts scheduled runs late, by up to an hour.
Push fails with 403 Settings → Actions → General → Workflow permissions → select Read and write permissions.

Privacy

On Google's free tier, prompts sent to Gemini may be used by Google and read by reviewers. The agent only sends public news headlines, your bio and your past public posts. Never put private, client or company information in your bio or settings.
