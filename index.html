Ever tried buying bread in Harare during a rush hour?

Imagine running to the corner tuckshop every single time someone asks you for the time, or the bread price. You would waste half your day walking back and forth and your legs would give up by noon.

That is why smart shop owners keep a small chalkboard right by the window with the current prices written on it. People walking past just glance at the board instead of knocking on the door and asking the shopkeeper directly.

In software, we call this caching, and it saves our servers from getting exhausted by the exact same questions over and over again.

Questions

Frequently asked

Do I need to be a programmer?

No. Setup means copying and pasting a few keys into the right boxes, and the guide shows each click. If you'd rather not, choose "Setup with me".

Does it cost anything to run?

No. It uses free tiers: GitHub Actions (about 30 of your 2,000 free minutes a month), Google Gemini and LinkedIn's official API.

Will the posts sound like me?

You describe yourself in one sentence, and every post is written in that voice. You can test with dry runs, which write a draft without posting, before going live. You can also edit or delete any post on LinkedIn as usual.

Is it safe? Who sees my passwords?

Nobody. Your copy runs in your own GitHub account, and your keys are stored as encrypted GitHub secrets that only your copy can use. It posts through LinkedIn's official API, using a developer app that you create and control.

What do I need to do after setup?

Every 60 days, LinkedIn asks you to renew your access token. It takes about 2 minutes, and GitHub emails you if it expires. That's it.

Can it post on my company page?

Not yet. It posts on your personal profile, which is where most reach comes from.
