Ever tried buying bread in Harare during a rush hour?

Imagine running to the corner tuckshop every single time someone asks you for the time, or the bread price. You would waste half your day walking back and forth and your legs would give up by noon.

FM
Commercial farmer, Mashonaland
Sample draft · Wednesday: practical tip

Clean your tractor fuel filters before every major planting push.

Waiting until a tractor sputters in the middle of a dry ploughed field wastes precious daylight and delays your whole schedule.

Sediment and water love to hide in diesel storage drums, and they always find their way into your machine at the worst possible moment.

Take ten minutes every single morning during the busy season to drain the water separator and check the primary filter element. Keep spare filters and the right wrenches in the service bakkie.

The payoff is simple: zero unexpected breakdowns when the soil moisture is right and every single hour truly counts.

How often do you check your fuel filters when the pressure is on?

Questions

Do I need to be a programmer?

No. Setup means copying and pasting a few keys into the right boxes, and the guide shows every click. If you'd rather not, choose "Setup with me".

I'm not in tech. Will it work for me?

Yes. You set your field, for example "real estate in Zimbabwe", "agriculture and farming", "accounting and tax" or "marketing", and every post follows it: the news, the tips, the tools, the debates and the lessons. Add your country to get local news.

Where does the news come from?

For your field, it searches Google News and Reuters for stories from the last few days. For tech, it reads TechCrunch, Wired, CNET, The Verge, Ars Technica, Reuters and Hacker News. You can add any other news feed you like. Posts only state what the headlines say, and never invent facts.

Will my posts look like everyone else's?

No. You set your own bio and niche, such as "fintech and mobile money" or "data analytics". Every topic and news story is picked for your field and written in your voice, and the news order is shuffled for each person, so two users rarely write about the same story.

Does it cost anything to run?

No. It uses free tiers: GitHub Actions (about 30 of your 2,000 free minutes a month), Google Gemini, and LinkedIn's official API.

Can I check the posts before they go out?

You can run a test whenever you like. It writes a draft without posting it. After it goes live, you can edit or delete any post on LinkedIn as usual.

Is it safe? Who sees my passwords?

Nobody. Your copy runs in your own GitHub account, and your keys are stored as encrypted GitHub secrets that only your copy can use. It posts through LinkedIn's official API, using a developer app you create and control.

What do I need to do after setup?

Every 60 days, LinkedIn asks you to renew your access token. It takes about two minutes, and GitHub emails you if it expires.

Can it post on my company page?

Not yet. It posts on your personal profile, which is where most reach on LinkedIn comes from.
